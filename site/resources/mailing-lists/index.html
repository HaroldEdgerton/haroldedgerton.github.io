Mailing Lists

Official and resident mailing lists for Edgerton.

Email Lists

We have two official house mailing lists, eh-announce and eh-events, and one resident mailing list, eh-open. The house officers, heads of house, and manager use the eh-announce list to pass along important community bulletins and the eh-events list to communicate house and campus events. The eh-open list is used by Edgerton residents to discuss community-related issues.

We try to keep the mailing list memberships current, but if you find yourself accidentally omitted from any of these lists, please visit the respective mailing list page and alter your subscription settings:

  • eh-announce
  • eh-events
  • eh-open

To organize and join pick up games of any kind, sign up for eh-sports.

Slack Workspace

Edgerton House has an official Slack workspace within the MIT enterprise where you can post move-out sales, discuss AC/heating issues, and see Edgerton House social events in one place. The Slack group will exist in addition to existing communication channels.

Slack workspace URL: mit-edgerton-house.slack.com

We will periodically add new residents to the Slack group, eh-open, and eh-announce. If you would like to be added sooner, please reach out to eha-secretary@mit.edu and eha-it@mit.edu.

Being a part of the Slack group means agreeing to adhere to the Edgerton House Slack group community guidelines.

If you would prefer to opt out of the Slack, please also reach out to eha-secretary@mit.edu and eha-it@mit.edu.
